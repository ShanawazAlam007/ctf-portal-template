TOURNAMENT DIRECTIVES

RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Strict compliance with these rules is mandatory for all participating competitors. Violations will result in disqualification.

PERMITTED ACTIONS

  • Target Reconnaissance: Active and passive port scanning, banner grabbing, and service enumeration strictly against designated challenge targets assigned in your mission briefs once authenticated.
  • Security Tooling: Use of standard penetration testing utilities including Nmap, Burp Suite, Gobuster, FFuf, SQLMap, curl, Python, Netcat, and CyberChef.
  • Service Exploitation: Discovering weaknesses, bypassing application filters, and elevating privileges strictly within designated challenge environments.
  • Legitimate Post-Exploitation: Reading configuration files, decoding historical archives, and retrieving authentic challenge flags.

STRICTLY PROHIBITED

  • No Attacks on Portal Infrastructure: Do NOT attack, scan, or fuzz the CTF portal web server, scoring engine, or database hosting this platform. The portal is an administrative management system, not a challenge target.
  • No Denial of Service: Do NOT launch volumetric floods (SYN floods, UDP storms, massive parallel HTTP fuzzers) that degrade service for fellow participants.
  • No Destructive Commands: Never alter system configurations or lock other participants out of challenge environments.
  • No Hypervisor / Cloud Escapes: Do NOT attempt to contact cloud Instance Metadata Services (IMDS) or compromise the underlying host virtualization infrastructure.
  • No Flag Sharing / Collusion: Submitting flags discovered by other competitors or publishing solutions online during the active competition is strictly prohibited.

FLAG SUBMISSION & TIE-BREAKING

Flag Structure: Flags follow the standard canonical format: CTF{...} unless otherwise specified in the briefing.

Tie-Breaking Mechanism: If multiple competitors achieve the same score, the competitor who reached that score first is ranked higher on the live leaderboard.

Hint Penalties: First clue for each challenge is free. Additional clues carry score point deductions. Competitors cannot unlock clues if their score balance is insufficient.
